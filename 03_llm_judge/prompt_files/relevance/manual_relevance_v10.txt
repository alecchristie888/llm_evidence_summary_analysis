Task: Determine whether the statement is relevant to answering the question above.
A statement is relevant if it addresses the same specific subject as the question and provides information that could contribute to answering it, even partially.

Mark No if:
- The statement is about a different species, taxon, or organism than the question asks about (e.g. a statement about moths when the question asks about bats, or a statement about invertebrates in general when the question asks about wild bees)
- The statement addresses a different intervention or mechanism than the question asks about (e.g. a statement about seal predation when the question asks about catch protection devices)

Mark Yes if:
- The statement directly addresses the subject of the question, even if it only partially answers it or addresses one side of a comparison
- The statement provides contextual or background information about the same subject and same taxa/intervention as the question
- The statement reports absence of evidence on the question's specific topic
- The statement addresses one aspect of a multi-part question (e.g. reports an outcome for one country or one study when the question asks for a comparison)

Important: Do not penalise a statement for being brief, vague, or incomplete. A short statement about the same subject is relevant even if it does not fully answer the question. Focus on whether the subject matches, not whether the statement is informative enough.

Provide your reasoning first, then give a final verdict of 'Yes' (relevant) or 'No' (not relevant).

  "statement": the statement text
  "reasoning": your explanation
